Act I

The Memory of Jade (翡翠の記憶)

She arrived from the Netherworld as a soft-spoken girl trying her best to communicate with humans. What unfolded over the years became one of the most passionate and genuine connections ever witnessed in digital entertainment.

「こんるし〜！今日もいっぱい愛してるよ！」

From whisper-soft ASMR streams that brought peaceful sleep to thousands, to legendary desk-slams and death-metal screams that became global cultural folklore, Rushia poured every ounce of her soul into her audience.

Dedicated to the global Fandead (ふぁんでっど 🦋) community.
